Data Sources and Accuracy

The PERM case data displayed on ImmiLane is sourced from publicly available records at the DOL's FLAG (Foreign Labor Application Gateway) system. While we strive to present this data accurately and update it multiple times daily, we make no guarantees regarding the completeness, accuracy, or timeliness of the information displayed.

Processing time estimates are generated algorithmically based on historical trends and current processing rates. These estimates are projections, not guarantees, and should not be the sole basis for any immigration-related decisions.
