Frequently Asked Questions

What is ImmiLane?
ImmiLane is a free PERM labor certification tracker that provides real-time processing data sourced from the U.S. Department of Labor. It helps applicants and immigration professionals estimate certification timelines and monitor case status trends.
Where does ImmiLane get its data?
All case data is sourced directly from the DOL FLAG (Foreign Labor Application Gateway) system at flag.dol.gov. ImmiLane scans for status updates multiple times per day and displays aggregated, anonymized statistics. No personal or employer-identifying information is stored or displayed.
How do I track my PERM case?
Open the ImmiLane app or visit immilane.com. Enter your PERM case number (format: G-100-XXXXX-XXXXXX or G-200-XXXXX-XXXXXX) to see your current case status and an estimated certification timeline based on historical processing patterns.
How accurate are the timeline estimates?
Estimates are based on historical DOL processing patterns, current backlog levels, and recent certification rates. While they provide a reasonable projection, actual processing times may vary due to audits, requests for information (RFIs), or changes in DOL staffing and priorities.
Is my personal information safe?
ImmiLane does not collect, store, or share any personal information. Case lookups are performed in real time against DOL public data. We do not require account registration or login to use the app.
What do the different case statuses mean?
  • Analyst Review — Your case is in the DOL queue awaiting review by an analyst.
  • Certified — The labor certification has been approved.
  • Denied — The application was not approved. You may appeal or refile.
  • Audit — DOL has selected the case for additional documentation review.
  • Withdrawn — The application was withdrawn by the employer.
The app is not showing my case. What should I do?
Make sure you are entering the correct case number format (G-100-XXXXX-XXXXXX or G-200-XXXXX-XXXXXX). If your case was recently filed, it may take a few days to appear in the DOL system. If the issue persists, contact us at support@immilane.com.
