Privacy Policy

Overview

ImmiLane ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website immilane.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you visit our site.

Information We Collect

We do not require account creation or login for the free site. We do not collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it through our feedback form (optional email address and message) or by subscribing to ImmiLane Case Watch (see below).

Case Watch Subscribers — Data We Collect

When you subscribe to Case Watch we collect and store: your email address, the PERM case number you register, your subscription plan and status, and a record of emails we sent you. Payment card details are collected and stored by Stripe, not by us; we receive only a payment status and the last four digits and card brand.

We also retain Stripe's identifiers for your subscription (customer and subscription IDs) and your billing status; these are kept after cancellation as part of our billing records.

We log delivery problems — bounces and spam complaints reported by our email delivery provider — and use them to stop sending to addresses that bounce or mark our email as spam.

Free samples. If you request a free sample email, we collect and store your email address and the case number you enter; these are used to build and send that sample and to limit repeat requests.

How Subscriber Data Is Used

Your email address and case number are used solely to monitor that case against published DOL data and to send you the emails you subscribed to, plus service messages about your subscription (payment failures, cancellation confirmations).

Subprocessors

We use the following service providers to operate Case Watch and the site:

  • Stripe — payment processing — receives your email address and the payment details you enter at checkout.
  • Resend — email delivery — receives your email address and message content.
  • Anthropic — AI-assisted generation of the narrative text in your emails — receives your case status data and your email address as part of the generation batch. All figures, dates and statuses in your email are rendered from our own records, not from the AI.
  • Cloudflare — hosting, DNS, and email routing.
  • Google Analytics / AdSense and Monumetric — analytics and advertising on the free public site only. No ads appear in Case Watch emails and these providers receive no subscriber data.

Marketing Email

Subscribers receive only transactional and service email relating to their subscription. We do not add subscribers to a marketing list. Every email includes one-click unsubscribe.

Analytics & Cookies

We use the following third-party services that may place cookies or collect anonymized usage data:

  • Google Analytics 4 (GA4) — Collects anonymized data about page views, scroll depth, time on page, and feature usage to help us improve the site. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy
  • Cloudflare Web Analytics — Privacy-focused analytics that do not use client-side state (cookies) to collect metrics. Cloudflare's privacy policy: cloudflare.com/privacypolicy

Advertising

We use Google AdSense to display advertisements. Google AdSense may use cookies and web beacons to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings or aboutads.info/choices.

Third-party vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on your visit to this site and/or other sites on the Internet. You may opt out of the use of the DART cookie by visiting the Google advertising privacy page.

Third-Party Advertising

This Site is affiliated with Monumetric (dba for The Blogger Network, LLC) for the purposes of placing advertising on the Site, and Monumetric will collect and use certain data for advertising purposes. To learn more about Monumetric's data usage, click here: Publisher Advertising Privacy

Feedback Form

Our optional feedback form collects:

  • Feedback category and message (required)
  • Email address (optional, only if you want us to follow up)

This data is stored securely and used solely to improve ImmiLane. We do not sell, share, or distribute your feedback data to third parties.

Data Sources

All PERM case data displayed on ImmiLane is sourced from publicly available records at FLAG.DOL.GOV. We do not collect or store individual immigration case details beyond what is publicly available.

Data Retention

Analytics data is retained according to the respective third-party service policies.

Subscriber records are retained while the subscription is active and for 12 months after cancellation, so you can restart without re-registering, and are then deleted. Billing records are retained by Stripe under its own policy, and by us as required by U.S. tax and accounting law (generally seven years). Feedback submissions are retained indefinitely unless deletion is requested.

Your Rights

You may:

  • Disable cookies in your browser settings
  • Opt out of Google personalized ads via Google Ads Settings
  • Request deletion of any feedback data you've submitted by contacting us
  • Unsubscribe from any Case Watch email using the one-click unsubscribe link in that email
  • Request a copy of your subscriber data by emailing support@immilane.com
  • Request deletion of your data, actioned within 30 days (note that deletion cancels any active subscription)

For California residents (CCPA/CPRA) and residents of the EEA and UK (GDPR): we do not sell or share personal information as those terms are defined under those laws.

Children's Privacy

ImmiLane is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, email support@immilane.com or use the feedback form on our site.
